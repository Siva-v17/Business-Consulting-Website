About Stackly

Independent counsel for leaders making decisions with no easy answer.

Founded by former operators, Stackly Consulting exists because most advisory work stops at the recommendation. We stay for the harder part — making it real.

Our story

Built by people who sat on the other side of the table first.

Stackly Consulting was started in 2014 by a small group of operators who had each run strategy or transformation functions inside the companies they later advised. That experience shapes everything about how we work: we know what a plan looks like when it survives contact with a real organisation, and what it looks like when it doesn't.

Today we're a team of 64 consultants across three offices, working in focused pods rather than sprawling account teams. Every engagement is led by a partner who stays hands-on from the first workshop to the final handover — not a rotating cast of juniors.

We take on a deliberately small number of clients each year so that every relationship gets the attention a hard decision deserves.

How we work

A five-step approach we don't skip, even under deadline.

The sequence stays fixed. What changes is how much time each step needs.

01

Diagnose before prescribing

Two to three weeks embedded with your team, reviewing data and sitting in the meetings where the real decisions get made.

02

Pressure-test the options

We build out the two or three viable paths, not one preferred answer, and show the trade-offs of each in numbers your CFO will accept.

03

Align the room

A working session with the full leadership team to get disagreement on the table before it becomes a delivery problem.

04

Build the plan with your people

Your own managers co-author the execution plan alongside us, so ownership doesn't have to be handed over after we leave.

05

Stay through the first 90 days

A partner remains on call through early execution to catch the problems that only show up once the plan meets reality.

Good to know

Questions we're asked before an engagement starts.

How many clients do you take on at once?

+

Each partner runs no more than three active engagements, so a team can move quickly without waiting in a queue behind other clients.

Do you only do strategy, or execution too?

+

Both. Every engagement includes a build phase where our team works alongside yours to get the plan running, not just written down.

What does a typical engagement cost?

+

It depends on scope and duration. We share a fixed-fee proposal after the first working session, once we understand the real size of the problem.

Can you work with an in-house strategy team?

+

Yes — a number of our engagements sit alongside an internal strategy function, focused on the parts that need outside perspective or extra capacity.
