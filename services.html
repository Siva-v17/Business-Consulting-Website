FAQ

Questions we hear before an engagement starts.

How long does a typical engagement run?

Advisory Sprints run 2–4 weeks for a single, well-defined question. Full Engagements typically run 8–20 weeks and cover diagnosis through implementation support. Embedded Partnerships are ongoing, usually starting at six months.

Do you work across more than one practice area at a time?

Most engagements draw on two or three practice areas at once — a cost transformation project, for example, usually touches Operating Model and Organisation & People together. We staff a single cross-practice team rather than handing off between departments.

Who actually does the work — partners or junior staff?

A named partner is accountable for the engagement from scoping to final readout. Analysts and associates support the workstreams, but the partner runs every major working session and sign-off.

How is an engagement priced?

Advisory Sprints are fixed-fee. Full Engagements are scoped and quoted after an initial diagnostic call. Embedded Partnerships run on a monthly retainer sized to the weekly capacity you need.

What happens after the engagement ends?

Every deliverable is built for handover — playbooks, models and decision rights are designed so your team can run the plan without us in the room. Many clients bring us back for a quarterly check-in rather than a full re-engagement.
