The engagement journey

Six steps from first conversation to embedded change.

Every step has a clear owner on both sides and a defined output — nothing moves forward on assumption.

01

Week 0

A no-obligation conversation to understand the problem and whether we're the right fit.

02

Week 1

03

Common questions

What clients usually ask before starting.

How long does a typical engagement run?

Most engagements run 8–20 weeks for the core diagnostic-through-mobilisation work, with roughly a third of clients continuing into an embedded partnership afterward.

Do you work with companies below a certain size?

We work with mid-market and enterprise organisations, generally from $15M in revenue upward. For earlier-stage companies, our advisory sprint model is usually the better fit.

How is a project priced?

Fixed-fee for defined scopes, retainer for embedded partnerships. We agree pricing during scoping — before any work begins — so there are no surprises mid-engagement.

Will the same team stay through the engagement?

Yes. A named partner and core team stay assigned from discovery through delivery — we don't rotate staff mid-project.

Can an engagement start with a smaller pilot?

Yes. Many clients start with an advisory sprint on one decision before scoping a larger fixed-fee project — it's a low-risk way to test working styles.

Do you work with our internal team or replace it?

We work alongside your internal team by design — capability transfer is part of every engagement so the plan survives after we leave.
